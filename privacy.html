Privacy Policy

Kumsorn works without an account. This policy explains on-device content, optional measurement and your privacy choices.

Apple and Meta also handle information under their own policies; RevenueCat processes registered purchase and attribution information for Kumsorn.

What Kumsorn stores

Kumsorn stores your language, quote categories, saved quotes, reminder choices, selected font, background, app icon, wellbeing entries, goal text, daily quote-use count, offer-display history and Premium entitlement locally on your device. Widget settings and selected content are copied to Apple’s on-device app-group storage so your widgets can display them. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple settings. Kumsorn does not provide its own account or cloud sync for this personal content. This includes your Lock Screen quote interval and active hours. These schedule preferences stay in local app and widget storage and are not sent to Kumsorn, Meta or RevenueCat.

What we do not collect

Kumsorn does not require an account and does not operate a Kumsorn server. The app does not upload your saved quotes, goals or wellbeing entries to Kumsorn, Meta or RevenueCat. It does not request your contacts or device location. We do not request your name or email address in the app. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond. Optional purchase and advertising measurement involves the identifiers and technical data described below; using the app without an account does not make those records fully anonymous.

Optional ad measurement

If you allow tracking in iOS, Kumsorn uses Meta’s SDK to measure app installs and Premium purchases resulting from our ads. It sends app activation and limited purchase or subscription events, such as a trial start, paid conversion or Lifetime purchase, with the product, amount and currency when available and device or app identifiers used for attribution. Meta’s SDK may also process technical analytics and crash data. In versions configured for server-side subscription reporting, Kumsorn registers verified Apple transactions with RevenueCat after tracking authorization. RevenueCat receives transaction information, a randomly generated customer ID, tracking-consent status and attribution identifiers and technical data, including an IP address. That customer ID can be associated with purchases and advertising identifiers; it is not a promise of anonymity. RevenueCat can send subscription starts, paid conversions, renewals and Lifetime events to Meta through the Conversions API even while the app is closed. We do not send saved quotes, goals, wellbeing entries, name, email or payment-card details in these measurement events.

Why information is used

We read Apple transaction and entitlement information to provide purchases, Restore and Premium access. Optional advertising measurement relies on your tracking permission and consent where required. It is not required to use free features, buy Premium or restore purchases. Support information is used to answer requests and resolve problems. Where a legal basis is required, providing purchased features and requested support may be necessary to perform our agreement with you; proportionate service administration, security and dispute handling may rely on legitimate interests, and legally required records on legal obligations. We do not use goals or wellbeing entries for advertising or make automated decisions about your eligibility for healthcare, employment, credit or similar services.

Notifications and widgets

If you choose reminders, Kumsorn asks Apple for permission and schedules quote notifications on your device. Widgets use the preferences and content selected on the same device. You can disable reminders or remove widgets in the app or iOS Settings. Denying notification permission does not enable tracking and does not prevent ordinary quote browsing.

Fonts and third parties

Kumsorn includes its font files inside the app and does not download fonts while you use it. Apple processes payments and handles your payment-account information; Kumsorn does not receive your card details. Meta provides optional ad measurement. RevenueCat provides subscription monitoring and the configured Meta integration for registered transactions. Their policies explain their own processing. RevenueCat’s data-processing terms also explain its role as Kumsorn’s service provider.

Your choices

A new user who does not allow tracking does not start Kumsorn’s Meta or RevenueCat advertising-measurement setup. Change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. After withdrawal, reopen Kumsorn with a network connection so it can stop its Meta events and send the updated consent status to RevenueCat if previously registered. This necessary consent-update connection may contact RevenueCat after permission is withdrawn. If delivery fails, the update must be retried; server-side Meta reporting stops when the provider receives and applies the update. Records already sent are not erased by this choice, and RevenueCat may continue monitoring previously registered subscriptions. Removing the app does not notify these providers of withdrawal, delete their existing records or cancel billing.

Overseas processing and safeguards

Your personal quote, goal and wellbeing content stays in on-device app and widget storage unless you choose to share it, back it up or include it in a support message. Purchase, attribution and support information handled by providers may be processed outside your country. RevenueCat states that its customer-data infrastructure is in the United States; Apple and Meta describe their international processing in their policies. Applicable service-provider terms and transfer safeguards are described in the linked provider information, including RevenueCat’s data-processing terms. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your request. Device protections and provider security measures reduce risk, but no system can promise absolute security.

Retention and data deletion

Local settings and personal content remain until you change them or delete the app, subject to Apple’s backup settings. Provider-held purchase records may be needed while registered subscriptions are monitored and for transaction verification, support, fraud prevention or disputes. Advertising records are retained for the stated measurement purposes and applicable provider requirements. We assess continued need by those purposes, whether the subscription or request remains active, and any legal retention obligation, rather than promise one period for every record. When records we control are no longer needed, we use the relevant provider’s deletion or de-identification process, subject to legal retention. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request, follow-up or a related dispute and meet legal obligations. Uninstalling or withdrawing tracking permission is not a provider-deletion request.

Access, correction, deletion and complaints

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, request a portable copy, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent without changing the lawfulness of earlier processing. Email KumsornOfficial@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request” and tell us what you need. We may ask for the minimum information needed to locate your records and verify the request, such as a Kumsorn purchase transaction or order reference. Do not send your Apple password, payment-card details, or wellbeing entries. We will explain identification limits, act on records we can identify and control through providers, and respond within the period required by applicable law. If a request cannot be fulfilled or information must be retained, we will explain why and any available next steps. You can raise a concern with us and, where applicable, complain to your local privacy regulator. Apple and Meta also offer privacy-request routes for information they control. Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription or request a refund; manage billing separately in your Apple Account settings.
