A few words worth keeping close

Kumsorn lets you browse quotes, choose topics and save words you want to read again. You can change the reading background and font. Options marked Premium require a purchase entitlement.

For encouragement without opening the app, add a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget. Every widget is free.

Meet Mochi

Mochi is Kumsorn’s little character. Choose a colour in the app and see Mochi during different moments of your day. Mochi is a character, rather than a chatbot that answers questions.

Start without a Kumsorn account

There is no Kumsorn account to create. Preferences, saved quotes, goals and check-ins are stored on your device. Restoring Premium uses the Apple Account that made the purchase; it is separate from recovering personal data stored locally.

What these pages cover

These articles were drafted with AI assistance and checked against product records, app screens and relevant Apple documentation. We do not invent author biographies or customer reviews. If a step differs on your device, please tell us.
