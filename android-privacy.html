Privacy Policy

Kumsorn is provided by Nopparin Saengsiripongpun. Contact KumsornOfficial@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

On your device

Kumsorn works without a Kumsorn account. It stores your preferences, saved quotes, goals and wellbeing entries locally. The Home Screen widget uses a local copy of public quote content, your language and your chosen mascot. Kumsorn excludes its private app data from Android backup and device transfer. Clearing app storage or removing Kumsorn can delete this data. Sharing a quote or contacting support sends the information you choose to share.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles payment and account details. Where Premium is available, RevenueCat processes purchase information, a randomly generated customer identifier, and technical information such as IP address to verify purchases and keep Premium and Restore Purchases working. Kumsorn does not receive your payment card details. Kumsorn does not send wellbeing entries, goals or saved quotes to these purchase providers. The Android app does not use this billing integration for advertising attribution.
