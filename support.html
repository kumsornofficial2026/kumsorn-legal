Support

Help with Kumsorn, Premium subscriptions, reminders, and widgets.

Contact us

Email KumsornOfficial@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Premium and purchases

Open Kumsorn, go to Settings → Plans, and choose Restore Purchases if an active subscription is not recognized. Apple handles payments, refunds, cancellations, and subscription management through your Apple Account.

Reminders and widgets

For reminders, confirm that notifications are enabled for Kumsorn in iOS Settings. For widgets, open Kumsorn once after installing or updating, then add or refresh the widget from the Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Data deletion and tracking choices

Kumsorn has no user account or Kumsorn server holding your saved quotes, goals, or settings. Delete the app to remove its local data, subject to your Apple device-backup settings. To stop future Meta ad measurement in versions that include it, turn off Kumsorn in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

For a request about Kumsorn-related ad measurement data already sent to Meta, email KumsornOfficial@gmail.com with the subject “Kumsorn data deletion request.” We will review what can be identified and help direct requests for data held by Meta. Do not email passwords or payment details. You can also use Meta’s Privacy Center to manage data Meta holds.
