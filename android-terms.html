Using Kumsorn

Kumsorn provides quotes, goals, personal reflection, optional reminders and a free Home Screen widget for personal use. The wellbeing check-in is for personal reflection. It does not provide a diagnosis, medical advice or treatment. Android manages the timing of widget updates and notifications.

Premium and billing

Premium unlocks additional categories and backgrounds. Monthly and Yearly renew automatically unless canceled in Google Play. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Review your local price and payment terms in Google Play before confirming. Restore Purchases checks eligible purchases under the original Google account. Purchases from Apple do not transfer to Google Play. Removing Kumsorn does not cancel billing.
